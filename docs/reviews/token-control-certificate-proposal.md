## Exact property and exclusions

Separate three claims, never collapse them into “ownership verified”:

1. **Observed controls at T:** supplied raw mint and complete account-frontier bytes from one finalized GMA bank T satisfy an exact named endpoint policy. Addresses, program owner, executable flag, integer amounts, layouts and authority roles bind explicitly. This is conditional on the declared capture trust; a hash proves record identity, not chain truth.
2. **Historical accounting to T:** complete bounded histories, canonical ordering and account lifetimes reconcile every ending and aggregate supply to that actual bank. Existing legacy implementation can establish its narrow reconciliation component. It does not establish every transient authority state or authenticate individual CPI effects.
3. **Proposed historical token-control property:** from a proved initialization boundary through end-of-bank T, all relevant mint/account data transitions have supported, completed semantics; no prohibited authority/extension/control operation is omitted, including a transient change restored before T. Define the allowed birth authority interval explicitly: initial mint/metadata authority may exist only for the proved birth issuance/revocation sequence; no freeze/delegate/external-close or unsupported extension powers. After revocation, the specified powers remain absent. This stronger certificate is **not implemented or established** by claims1/2 or endpoint matching.

The frontier includes zero-balance, closed/recreated and transient initialized accounts. Its closure is an obligation, not a top-holder approximation. A supply total cannot hide an omitted control history. Certificate scope ends at T; it says nothing about later transactions, freshness at entry, beneficial/common wallet ownership, wallet signer authenticity, funding attribution, bundle exposure, market fairness, recipient permission, rent/fee correctness, pool safety or sellability.

All proposed diagnostic output must keep lifecycle/authentication/ownership/trading approval false. A separately reviewed future trust contract may discharge named proof obligations; this report does not authorize adding an approval field or connecting a research result to entry.

## Minimum trust and noninterference obligations

Explicit assumptions must identify approved network/genesis/source, commitment/finality trust, complete address-query filter and exhaustion semantics, historical deployment/runtime identity where semantic proofs are used, and which facts remain only provider declarations. Current ProgramData after S cannot prove the overwritten binary at S. IDL/source revision pins are references, not deployed executable identity. Same-provider corroboration is not independence.

For the stronger token-control property, prove ownership enforcement prevents a non-token parent from directly modifying token-owned data **while that ownership holds**. Also cover the earlier System-owned allocation interval, close/recreate/reassign lifetimes, all CPIs reaching token handlers and any relevant runtime synchronization failure. Prove no omitted mint/account initialization can escape the frontier. A parent that catches an authority CPI failure must not be treated as having completed the requested write merely because its outer instruction succeeds.

For each relevant parent write, one of these reviewed obligations must be discharged: (a) it cannot alter the token-control property or invalidate its evidence; (b) its effect is modeled and proved; or (c) the certificate stays unknown. Current endpoint agreement, net lamport agreement, instruction preorder, event declarations and mayhem=false cannot discharge these obligations.

It is unnecessary to demand every historical parent-owned byte merely to state a token-data property **if** a slot-applicable noninterference proof covers that domain. Conversely, fee/global/native/rent/recipient histories are separate entry/economic dependencies unless they affect the control proof, initialization completion or frontier coverage. They cannot be silently waived when relevant. No authenticated Pump caller/deployment proof currently supplies this shortcut for the saved sample.

## Feasible next unit and stop conditions

**Available now, after coordinator dispatch:** a disconnected, read-only *obligation inventory* joining existing legacy history/snapshot diagnostic outputs to their exact persisted evidence hashes. It must distinguish observed endpoint policy, reconciled accounting and unproved historical controls; preserve false common-control/entry flags; report each missing obligation without claiming a new certificate. It can use existing accepted fixtures and APIs, so no provider or historical-S prerequisite is needed. This is a new reviewable projection, not acquisition recovery, log/signature joining or entry feature work owned by workers01/03/07/10. It must replay evidence rather than trust caller summaries. No implementation is authorized by this proposal.

**Not yet implementable as a positive Token-2022 certificate:** authenticated deployment interval/handler/caller/runtime semantics and approved evidence-contract equivalence are absent. Preflight these before charged history acquisition. Existing one-transaction raw diagnostic API also does not provide multi-record historical acquisition. An actual T capture must be labeled honestly and rejected by an S-only contract rather than relabeled to pass.

Existing legacy multi-account fixture demonstrates three-account reconciliation at13 charged attempts and a zero-request terminal retry; it proves a narrow component opportunity, not live/common ownership completion. For any candidate, budget is original used + setup + every history page/raw follow-up/order block/chunk/failure≤18; unlimited frontier/history has no finite completeness guarantee. Preserve remaining unknown on exhaustion, without retry resets, hidden setup or a new investigation ID.

## Acceptance and negative tests for a future dispatched unit

Reuse existing fixtures; add tests only for the new projection's distinct claims:

- Positive legacy13-request fixture exposes exact raw evidence refs, observed endpoint controls and historical accounting, but marks stronger control/noninterference/authentication/entry obligations unresolved. All approval fields false; repeated read does no I/O/writes.
- Missing/tampered/foreign evidence hash, rebound seal, mismatched counter or caller-fabricated reconciled summary cannot promote any obligation. Existing acquisition/seal tests remain with worker01; projection tests only its consumption boundary.
- Missing account history, mint-only coverage, new frontier account, transient closed/recreated account, cursor gap, duplicate/conflicting transaction or ambiguous same-slot ordering remains unknown despite matching supply.
- Active delegate with zero allowance, orphan allowance, freeze/owner mismatch, external close authority and malformed layouts fail the named policy. Owner-equal explicit close follows legacy policy but fails Token-2022 candidate policy; stale None payload is not a delegate.
- Transient control change then restoration never becomes “absent throughout” from a clean T endpoint. Caught/post-log CPI failure, raw/parsed disagreement or unproved parent-write noninterference cannot satisfy completion.
- Token-2022 endpoint-profile match alone remains diagnostic; unknown/wrong-class/duplicate TLVs and mutable/redirected metadata fail. Do not rerun parser implementation tests merely for count; test projection refusal to promote their result.
- Truthful later T and changed supply/burn evidence remain distinct from S/birth state. Wrong slot/signature, substituted confirmed later capture or fixed-supply fabrication cannot satisfy missing S obligations.
- Zero native residual, fee-return arithmetic or complete event prefix never authorizes controls, recipients or entry. Missing deployed-code trust cannot be filled by an official IDL hash.

Historical control certificate acceptance itself additionally needs independently reviewed fixtures for each discharged semantic/noninterference obligation and its failure counterpart, plus obtained trusted inputs. This list is a proposal for acceptance criteria, not executed tests or permission to relax gates.
