# L24 — Linked-wallet clustering (true controlled supply)

BASE `origin/integration/r1` (8c64fc8). Branch `cloud/L24`. PAPER ONLY, RECORDING ONLY: nothing here is read by `lean.strategy`.
No change to `desk/`. `lean/wallet_signals.py` is untouched (no hook was needed: the collector reads L13's stored row and events).

## Why
Research on pump.fun launches (MELT / MemeTrans, ~41k launches) reports that coordinated accounts hold about 36.5 % of supply, and
that linking accounts by shared non-exchange funders, shared Jito bundles and same-transaction co-occurrence, plus bundle statistics,
is among the most predictive signals. Our per-buyer L13 figures did not separate winners from losers, so this records holdings per
cluster instead.

## Design (`lean/clusters.py`)
One `observations(kind='clusters')` row per screened candidate (first PASS/REJECT decision = `decision_ts`, the L13 definition).
The row holds canonical JSON in `raw` and the figures in `meta`.

**Graph, as of the decision.** Nodes:
- the top-N holders (default 20). These are the owners of the largest token accounts, with the pool vault, burn accounts and
  pool-owned accounts excluded.
- the early buyers: L13's `buyer_wallets` plus the buyers of the examined window transactions.
- the creator: `coin_creator` of the pool account that the screen retained.

Edges:
| edge | source |
|---|---|
| `shared_funder` | Two or more nodes whose first funding transfer came from the same funder. Funders come from L13's buyer→funder map plus bounded lookups of the creator and the largest holders. A detected **hub** never links anything: a funder of buyers of ≥ `hub_min_mints` distinct mints within `hub_window_s` before the decision, counted from L13 `wallet_funding` and our own `cluster_funding` events. `ignore_funders` entries never link either. |
| `transfer` | A node funded by another node, a wallet-to-wallet token move (the pool balance is unchanged), or a SOL transfer between nodes inside an examined window transaction. |
| `same_slot` | First buys in the same slot of the first pool window, or pre-decision transactions of two examined holders in one slot. A slot shared by more than `same_slot_max_group` (8) wallets is treated as a crowd and does not link. |
| `jito_bundle` | A run of ≥ 2 **adjacent** pool signatures in one slot where one transaction pays one of the 8 Jito tip accounts (`jito_tip_accounts`, overridable). |
| `same_tx` | The signers and buyers of one transaction (co-signers, or a wallet buying for another), and a signature shared by two examined holders' histories. |

**Clusters** are the connected components (union-find). Recorded fields:
- `cluster_count`: components with ≥ 2 nodes.
- `largest_cluster_supply_pct`, `top_cluster_holders` (count), and `largest_cluster_wallets` (the holder addresses).
- `creator_cluster_supply_pct`: the creator plus everything linked to it. When the creator is unlinked this is the creator's own share.
- `linked_supply_pct_total`: all holders in clusters of size ≥ 2.
- `jito_bundled_buys`.
- `fresh_wallet_share`: the share of examined holders whose first transaction falls within 24 h before the decision. It is decided
  from one bounded history page, and `fresh_checked` gives the number of holders it covers.

The row also carries:
- `clusters` (up to 20, with members, supply share and edge types), `edge_counts`, and `edges` (capped at 200).
- `lookups` (funder, its source, freshness), `hub_funders`, `holders_source`, `supply_source`, `signals_row`, `calls`.
- `decision_ts`, `collected_at`, `late_s`, `window_after_decision_s`, `flags`, `unavailable`, `partial`.

Every null has a reason in `unavailable`. Shares are of the TOTAL supply. The caveat that shared funding is a signal and not proof
of common ownership is written into every row.

**No lookahead:**
- Holders come from the screen's own `getTokenLargestAccounts` body (`screen:holders`, written before the decision;
  `holders_source='screen'`).
- Only a candidate whose screen never reached its holder stage reads the holders at collection. Every holder-based field and
  `cluster_count` is then flagged `AT_COLLECTION`.
- The pool window ends at `min(graduation + window_s, decision_ts)` by block time.
- Holder histories count only signatures at or before the decision. A funding transfer is the wallet's first transaction, and only
  when that transaction is at or before the decision.
- Hub evidence is limited to events stamped at or before the decision.
- The supply is the screen's. Otherwise one `getTokenSupply` call is made and the row is flagged `rpc_after_decision`.

**Learner (`lean/learn.py`).** The 7 figures are added as `CLUSTER_FEATURES`, with `cluster_values` following the same guards:
- version `clusters_v1` only;
- `_pick` takes the last row at or before the decision;
- `AT_COLLECTION` is accepted only within `max_late_s`, and any other flag is excluded;
- unflagged holder figures must come from the screen snapshot;
- `jito_bundled_buys` is used only when the window was cut at the decision;
- supply shares are used only with the screen supply.

`runner_changes` emits `wait_for_clusters:<names>`. These changes are additive: the L19 mutation harness still kills 33/33.

**Wiring (small delimited hooks):**
- `lean/__main__.py`: `clusters` key (None = OFF), strict validation at load, and the build.
- `lean/runner.py`: `self.clusters`, health `clusters` (via `_aux_health`, so it has `alive`), its own daemon thread `clusters`, and
  the shared `AUX_JOIN_S` stop deadline.
- `config/lean/lean.example.json` enables it.
- `tests/lean/fakeworld.py` gains optional `holder_rows` (planted holder token accounts) and nothing else changes.

## Budget
- **Shared low lane, L13's discipline.** `stop` is checked before every request. A call is made only while `reserve_tokens` (1)
  more tokens stay for the path recorder, L11, L12F and L13. A `no_token` shed is paced at 0.5 s for up to `shed_wait_s` on its own
  thread, with no lock held. A shed window, or running out of patience, ends the candidate with what it has learned; if nothing was
  learned yet, the candidate is retried later. Credit shedding (L15) sheds the shared low lane, so it reaches this collector as well.
- **Hard cap `max_calls`, default 20**, counting requests actually sent.
- **Calls per candidate.** The read order is: owners (1); then lookups for the creator and the 5 largest holders (1 history page
  each, plus 1 funding transaction when the history is complete and L13 does not already know the funder); then the pool signatures
  (1–2); then 4 window transactions. With the screen's holders and supply (every PASS) the most this takes is 19. The worst case is
  21, so the cap cuts the last window read.
- **At ~70 candidates/h:** at most 1,400 calls/h (≈0.39 req/s), and typically about 900–1,300 because L13 already knows some
  funders and old wallets truncate the history page.
- **The rest of the low lane at that rate:**
  - path recorder: ~420 live paths → 9 batched calls per 15 s ≈ 2,160/h;
  - L13: ≤ 1,120/h;
  - L12F: ≤ 210/h.

  With clusters added, the worst case is about 4,900/h against the low lane's 7,200/h (10 req/s × 0.20).
- **Credits:** at 1 credit per call (ops `DEFAULT_COSTS`), clusters add at most about 34k credits/day (about 1.0M/month). The
  production `ops.credit_budget_month` is null, so nothing caps this on credits. Set a budget before enabling it on a small plan.

## Limitations
- **These are signals, not ownership.** A shared funder can be an undetected service. Hub detection needs ≥ 5 mints of evidence, so
  it is weak just after a fresh deploy. A same-slot pair can be two independent snipers.
- **Launch bundles on the bonding curve are not seen.** Bundle detection sees only the first POST-graduation pool window (4
  transactions) and only pool-touching transactions; a tip paid in a non-pool transaction of the bundle is missed. Most bundling
  happens on the bonding curve, which this does not page because the mint's history is too long. Bonding-curve coordination shows up
  only through the funding, shared-slot and shared-transaction edges of the examined holders.
- **Funding and freshness cover at most 6 wallets** (the creator plus the 5 largest holders); smaller holders join a cluster only
  through window, bundle or transfer edges or L13's funder map. A busy wallet's history page fills with post-decision transactions,
  so its funder is unknown (`HISTORY_TRUNCATED`) and its age is decided only when even that page is older than 24 h.
- **Snapshot granularity.** The holder snapshot is the screen's top 20 token accounts. The owners of those accounts are resolved at
  collection; a token-account owner change between the screen and the collection is not detected.
- **Lag.** It waits for L13's row (up to `signals_wait_s`) so that it can reuse the funder map, so a row typically lands one or two
  minutes after the decision. The figures are as of the decision, but a rule using them needs the runner to wait
  (`requires_runner_change`).
- **Nothing is validated against outcomes yet.** That is what this data is for (the learner tables, then L19 proposals).
