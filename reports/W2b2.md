## Fixes

1. **HIGH, prefetched rows are a different feature.** A prefetched window ends 26-38 s before the decision, so its figures are smaller (medians
   x0.58-0.63 in the review). Every prefetched row now carries a distinct `version` (`features_v3p` / `flow_v1p` / `risk_external_v1p` / wallet and
   clusters `...p`) and `window_mode='prefetch'`. The learner reads a `p` version only for a prefetch row and the plain one only for a decision
   row, builds **separate non-pooled columns** (`name@p`), reports `window_modes` per kind, and never pools them with pre-W2b rows under
   `--all-configs` (the config hash differs, and the version check rejects a mixed pool). A prefetched features row records which fields are
   prefetch-measured (`prefetch_fields`): only those are split off, snapshot fields stay plain. The staleness is shrunk with the lead times (below).
2. **MED, snapshot time.** Holder snapshots (features top1/top10, clusters holder list) are stamped (`snapshot_at`, the time the read ran, not the
   write). The learner skips a snapshot older than `--max-snapshot-age-s` (default 45 s, `LookaheadPolicy.max_snapshot_age_s`) before the decision.
3. **MED, flow prefetch only where it is usable, on its own budget.** The shadow probe now judges the market bands with the live SOL price
   (`sol_usd` hook): a probable soft reject (the bands) skips the flow prefetch (flow is only collected for a PASS); the features job fetches the
   holder list only for a soft reject, and clusters never take the probe's holder list. The flow prefetch has its own hourly budget
   (`prefetch.flow_calls_per_hour`) so it cannot starve the post-screen flow budget; `health.flow.prefetch_calls_last_hour` and
   `prefetch.flow_gate` report it.
4. **MED, hazard paths are not coverage.** Example config ships `hazard_path_sample: 0.1`; the storage estimate is corrected (about 120 MB/day of
   `path_marks` at 0.2, 60 MB/day at 0.1). In the learner, hazard paths are no longer in the `paths` total and hazard skips go to their own counts, so
   coverage figures describe entered candidates only.
5. **MED, rollback signals.** `prefetch.probe_fp_rate` (shadow-screen false positives: said hazard-free, the real screen rejected it; with
   fp/fn/tn/tp counts) with a documented rollback threshold of 10 %; `low_lane` in runner health (tokens, sheds, backoffs); `held_risk` counts
   `shed_no_token` and `shed_backoff` separately, so a lane shared with the prefetch shows who starves.
6. **LOW, `take()` is single-use** (a second take returns `ALREADY_USED`, so two collectors or a retried pass cannot write the same job twice).
   A job still running at the decision is marked `cancelled` and its late result is dropped, not written over; the health double count of a
   cancelled job is removed.
7. **LOW, `_discover` reads the store outside the lock** (no db read under `Prefetch._lock`; the decision hook can never wait for a query).
8. **LOW, prefetched raw bodies carry a candidate id.** Wallet-signal and RugCheck raw bodies are buffered in the job and written at commit with
   the candidate id; a body that cannot be attached is skipped, not written anonymous.

## Interaction with W2a2 (found at the merge)
`origin/integration/r1` now holds the W2a2 arms. Its `PrefetchWorld` test failed after W2b2 #2/#3: a clusters prefetch job now reads its OWN holder
list (not the probe's), which had made its figures `AT_COLLECTION`, and the arms read `AT_COLLECTION` as "known after the decision". A job's read
is BEFORE the decision, stamped by `snapshot_at`, so such a row now has `holders_source='prefetch'` and no `AT_COLLECTION` flag (the learner already
accepts source `prefetch` only with `late_s <= 0`, and applies the snapshot age). `arms.py` is unchanged. Pinned by `test_w2b2` and a new mutant.

## Readiness vs staleness (`tests/lean/w2b_measure.py`, only HTTP faked)

"Ready" = a value observed before the screen decision, of hazard-free candidates (flow: PASS only). Staleness = decision minus the value's as-of.

| setting | load | readiness | staleness mean / max | prefetch/decision median ratios |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W2b defaults (lead 25-30 s, settle 4 s) | normal | 100 % | 29.8 s / 37.9 s | 0.58-0.63 |
| W2b2 defaults (risk 25, features 16, wallet 16, clusters 14, flow 15 s, settle 2 s) | normal | 100 % | 17.9 s / 23.2 s | 0.74-0.83 |
| W2b2 defaults | stress (1.5x latency, 5 % failures, 30 s 429 window) | flow 68 %, others 87-100 % | 14.4 s / 22.6 s | n/a |

The new leads trade about 12 s of staleness for the flow readiness in the stress world (68 %; the old defaults gave 92-100 % at 30 s of staleness).
The ratio still is not 1, which is why #1 keeps the prefetch columns apart: the learner never has to assume they match.

Overload limit (documented): at about 8x production arrival and 3x latency the prefetch cannot keep up and may cost the fallback a few usable
points; `reserve_tokens` can be raised. Watch `low_lane` and `probe_fp_rate`.

## Cost and storage (corrected)
- `hazard_path_sample` 0.2: about 120 MB/day of `path_marks`; 0.1: about 60 MB/day. The W2b estimate of 16 MB/day was about 7x low.
- Prefetch calls: about +0.9 aux calls per candidate; the flow gate and the soft-reject skip remove some of the flow calls of the W2b design.

## Rollback signals
- `prefetch.probe_fp_rate` > 0.10 for an hour: set `prefetch.probe=false` or `prefetch.enabled=false` (the post-screen path is untouched).
- `held_risk.shed_no_token` rising while `low_lane` is empty: raise `reserve_tokens` or disable the prefetch.
- `paths.hazard_path_sample` -> 0 stops new hazard paths; stored ones stay and are ignored by the learner tables.

## Residual risks
- The arms (W2a) that read collector rows by write time see the prefetch rows only after the commit; they must read `decision_ts`, not the write time.
- Wallet signals and clusters still run post-screen on hazard rejects (a cost, not a correctness issue).
- Prefetch figures remain smaller than decision-time ones; only the separate columns keep that from biasing a model.
- Old (pre-W2b) stored rows have no `window_mode`; the learner treats a missing mode as `decision`.
