# T24 — Throughput/latency headroom (F7, F9, F14)

Base: `origin/cloud/T22`. Python 3.12 (`.venv`). Fixtures only; no network, keys, VPS or production DB.

## What changed
- **F7** `desk/history_preparation_rejection.py`: `_attempts` no longer decodes every evidence page per retained
  rejection. A process-level incremental page index (keyed by db path/dev/inode) decodes each page once, keeps only
  64-hex keys by scan id (attempts) and by intent id (outcomes), and is rebuilt from scratch if the cached prefix
  (row count, max rowid, hash at the watermark) no longer matches. Used attempt pages are re-loaded and re-checksummed
  on every call; a conflicting or ambiguous page added later is still refused; a corrupt new page fails every call.
  The hard 4096-page / 256 MiB latch is replaced by a 100 000-page bound with a logged warning at 80 %
  (`WARN_INDEXED_PAGES`) as rotation guidance. Per-page scalar bounds and the 256 MiB compressed total are kept.
- **F9** `desk/paper_cycle.py`: `pass_deadline(cfg)`. Absent keys keep the strict 10 s. Opt-in:
  `paper_pass_deadline_version: 1` + `paper_pass_deadline_seconds` (int, 10..30). Anything else raises. Price/quote TTLs
  (`max_age_seconds=10`) are untouched. Per-request transport timeouts stay capped at 15 s (`MAX_REQUEST_TIMEOUT`,
  `_capped`). A deadline overrun after a charge is closed by the T22 closure (tested: no NULL pass, next cycle not
  `OBSERVATION_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`).
- **F14** `desk/monitoring_budget.py::_accounting`: the contiguous completed prefix of reservations is verified once and
  bound by a SHA-256 over its SQL tuples plus a binding digest of the context (expected tuple, grant, handoff, successor
  history). The hint lives in an untrusted sidecar `<evidence>.monitoring-verified.json`. Each call recomputes the tuple
  digest (no blob I/O), fully verifies rows beyond the prefix, and re-verifies a rotating slice of 4 prefix rows.
  Missing/forged/foreign sidecar => full verification. `zlib.error` on a retained blob now maps to
  `MONITORING_ACCOUNTING_INVALID` instead of escaping.

## Benchmarks (same fixture, synthetic rows/pages; BASE = origin/cloud/T22)
| case | BASE | AFTER |
|---|---|---|
| monitoring snapshot, 1 000 rows | 0.53 s | 0.05 s (cold 0.30 s once) |
| monitoring snapshot, 12 000 rows (~7 d held) | 2.28 s | 0.10 s (cold 2.6 s once) |
| gate, R=1, 300 extra pages | 0.26 s | 0.12 s |
| gate, R=1, 3 000 extra pages | 1.19 s | 0.17 s (cold 0.93 s once per process) |

Residual linear terms: SQL tuple digest (~8 µs/row) and `count/sum` over pages. Accounting is called ~5x per pass, so
7 days of held monitoring costs ~0.5 s instead of ~11 s.

## Limitations / for the coordinator
- Index and sidecar are derived hints: a page or blob mutated after caching and not on a used path is only caught by
  the rotating audit slice (monitoring) or the next rebuild (rejection). The blobs are content-addressed and append-only.
- Cold start costs one full pass per process (rejection) and once after upgrade (monitoring sidecar).
- A 30 s pass needs held/dispatcher `TimeoutStartSec` > 30 s (held unit was 20 s). Not changed (unit files not owned).
- Changing `desk/` changes the implementation hash: fresh stores only, or a reviewed transition.
- The three BASE failures above should be updated to the T22 closure semantics (T22F).
