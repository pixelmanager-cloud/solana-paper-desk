## Limitations
* A Helius JSON-RPC `error` body surfaces as `ValueError("Helius RPC error; check plan, quota and request support")` from
  `desk/providers.py` (not my file). It cannot be told from an integrity error, so the dispatcher (and a cycle) holds after it.
  If the coordinator wants those closed, `helius_rpc` should raise a typed error with `code='RPC_ERROR'` (already on the list).
* `EVENT_READER_SIZE_LIMIT` and `QUOTE_DEMAND_LIMIT` (oversized event/demand) are deliberately not on the allow-list (T01 keeps
  them category (b)); a candidate that hits them latches the store until a reviewed disposition.
* `HISTORY_RECOVERY_REQUIRED` is allow-listed: it is the history pager's own `RETRYABLE_ERROR` state (judgement call).
* The hold sentinel is a file next to the evidence DB; if even that cannot be written (disk full/read-only) nothing can protect
  the pass, and no recovery can write either. Sentinels are never cleaned automatically.
* Owner proof needs `/proc/locks` and `/proc/<pid>/fd` (Linux) or the injected equivalents; elsewhere "unknown" closes nothing.
