# T24R — durable, verifiable incremental state (F7, F14, F9) on top of T22G

Branch `cloud/T24R`, base `origin/cloud/T22G` (built fresh; the T24 commits were not used). Paper only. `desk/` changed, so the
implementation hash changes (rotate to a new ledger when flat).

## What changed
* **F7** `desk/verified_index.py` (new) + `history_preparation_rejection.py` + `paper_cycle_no_entry.py`: append-only, trigger-guarded
  `paper_verified_receipts`, written in the same transaction as each retained receipt after its full proof. A gate fully replays
  unindexed receipts plus `SAMPLE`=8 per kind (deterministic, moves with the pass count) and only binds the rest. `DESK_GATE_FULL_REPLAY=1`
  or `gate(..., full=True)` replays all. The attempt inventory of a preparation is read from the pages between its intent and outcome
  (rowid window); the 4096-page / store-wide latch is gone. 80 % rotation warnings for evidence pages (advisory 100,000) and bytes
  (the store's 256 MiB budget) go to the `.publish-refused.jsonl` beside the store (`read_refusals`) and the log.
* **F14** `monitoring_budget.py`: `paper_monitor_accounting_chain` (not `paper_monitoring_*`, which the terminal gate whitelists),
  hash-chained, appended in `retain_outcome`'s transaction; accounting recomputes the chain from SQL scalars and re-proves from blobs
  only unchained rows and `CHAIN_SAMPLE`=2 chained rows.
* **F9** `paper_cycle.py`: opt-in `paper_cycle_deadline_seconds` (absent = 10 s, unchanged), range 10..20, refused at load otherwise;
  per-request timeout and the collector deadline are clamped to 15 s (a source refuses more); `worst_case_seconds` with the T36 cadences
  (arithmetic table in `docs/PERFORMANCE_BUDGETS.md`). An overrun is the allow-listed `CYCLE_DEADLINE_UNAVAILABLE` => FAILED_CHARGED.
* **Benchmarks** `tools/research/bench_history_scale.py` (fixture builder + harness, runs unchanged on the base commit).
* Tests: `test_verified_index` (46), `test_monitoring_chain` (16), `test_cycle_deadline` (21), `test_bench_history_scale` (4); three T09
  audit tests flipped from `expectedFailure` to passing (F7 x2, F14).

## Limitations
* Outside the sample an indexed receipt's original attempt pages are not re-read; a later alteration is caught when it is sampled (every
  receipt over time) or by a full replay. The outcome page is always re-hashed. Rowid order of `pages` is relied on: a renumbered store fails closed.
* Chain recomputation is linear in reads (SQL scalars only, ~0.1 ms/read); blob loads are constant. Rows completed by `_resolve_abandoned` and rows of
  older stores have no chain link and are proved from blobs every time.
* The held-leg read count (5+1) in `CYCLE_REQUESTS` is an estimate from call sites; the 240 candidates/day, 20 pages, 576 reads/day are planning assumptions.
* The healthcheck does not read the rotation warnings yet (`tools/ops` is outside OWNS); they are in the refusal log via `read_refusals`.
* A longer deadline does not relax the 10 s price/quote freshness rule (stale reads are rejected, allow-listed, no latch).
