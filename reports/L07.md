# L07 — Price-path recorder

Branch `cloud/L07`, base `origin/integration/r1` plus `origin/cloud/L01..L05` (merged, no conflicts).

## What
`lean/paths.py` records a price path for every candidate whose screen shows no hazard, entered or not, for `path_hours` (6 h) every
`interval_s` (15 s), into the existing store as `observations` rows (append-only, `code_version` + `strategy_version` on each):
* `path_start`: screen features, `entered`, the not-entered reason (screen reasons, `QUOTE_FAILED`, the strategy's skip reason such as
  `FULL`), and the pool vault targets (so `resume()` can continue after a restart).
* `path_mark`: `ts, price_sol, liquidity_sol, slot, base_raw, quote_raw, status (OK/ACCOUNT_MISSING/EMPTY_POOL/MALFORMED), n`.
* `path_end`: marks, gaps, why.

One batched `getMultipleAccounts` per call; own non-sleeping token bucket (4 req/s = 40 % of 10); a transient provider error (429/5xx/
timeout) pauses the recorder 30 s, doubling to 10 min; the missed marks are counted as gaps (never retried at once). Every public method
(`start`, `poll`, `resume`) swallows and counts errors: a recorder failure cannot reach the trader.

Hazard filter: tracked when the screen passed, or failed ONLY for `MARKET_CAP_BELOW_MIN / MARKET_CAP_ABOVE_MAX / LIQUIDITY_BELOW_MIN`
and the market features are valid. Any other reason (authorities, extensions, pool/vault evidence, holders, provider errors) is not tracked.

Runner hooks (`lean/runner.py`, +21/-2): optional `path_recorder=None`; `_track()` (exception-safe) at screen failed, quote failed (then
re-raises), decision not enter, entered; a `paths` thread in `run()` stopped by the runner's stop event.

## Deviations (please read)
* **50 pools per call, not 100.** The spec says 100 pools / 250 pools → 3 calls, but each pool needs two vault accounts and
  `getMultipleAccounts` takes at most 100 accounts, so 250 pools = 5 calls of 100 accounts. The provider layer enforces the RPC limit.
* The pool-account fee amounts are taken from the screen features (not re-read), so liquidity uses the fees as of screening; raw reserves
  are stored so analysis can re-price.
* Raw account bytes are not retained (about 30 KB per 100-account poll, 6 h × 15 s × many pools); the reserves and slot are.
* Pricing mirrors `lean.candidates._market`; the quote vault is the pool's WSOL ATA, the base vault the pool's ATA for the screened token program.

## Evidence
Tests: `.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests/lean -p test_paths.py` (41 tests, fixtures + fake Helius + real Store).
Whole lean suite: `.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests/lean` (256 tests; OK in 2 of 3 runs, see limitations).
Fail-first: the tests were written against the module; a real bug was caught by them before commit (RED): after `resume()` the mark counter
restarted at 1 (`[1, 1]` instead of `[1, 2]`); GREEN after counting stored marks.
Mutation: 24 single-point mutants of `lean/paths.py` (hazard inversion, any/all, no backoff growth, no reset, 100 pools per call, no window
end, no shed, price without virtual quote, liquidity without fee, resume of ended paths, mark counter, duplicates, capacity, backoff, gap
accounting, status classification, owner/mint/program checks, cap, bucket, spendable>gross, error counting, exception swallowing): one
survived (resume skipping early-ended paths) → test `test_a_path_closed_early_is_not_resumed`; then 0 survivors. 6 mutants of the runner
hooks: 0 survivors (one hung the test until the thread was made daemon).

## Limitations
* `tests/lean/test_runner.py::Lifecycle::test_stop_joins_both_loops_and_writes_final_health` failed once in three full runs (L05's test, timing;
  the recorder is `None` there). Not touched.
* Never run against a live RPC; the fake Helius mirrors the `lean.providers.Helius.get_multiple_accounts` return shape.
* Paths of tokens with a Token-2022 transfer fee are priced from raw reserves like any other (no fee adjustment).

## For the coordinator
* **L09:** pass the recorder a DEDICATED client: `lean.paths.dedicated_helius(key)` (own limiter, `max_attempts=1`). With the shared client a
  429 on recorder traffic would block the trader's limiter.
* Wire `path_recorder=PathRecorder(store=…, helius=dedicated_helius(key), code_version=…, strategy_version=…)` into `Runner`; the recorder
  and runner both write to the same `Store` (it has its own lock).
* `resume()` is called by `run()`; the recorder does not close paths on shutdown, so a restart continues them.
* Volume estimate: 6 h × 240 marks/h = 1,440 marks per path; 100 concurrent paths ≈ 3.5 M mark rows per day (each about 400 bytes). Lower
  `path_hours` or raise `interval_s` if the store grows too fast; this is a judgement call left to you.
