## Limitations (honest)
- The window walk assumes `raw_events` is appended in receipt order; skew beyond the 600 s grace ends it early (fewer candidates, never an error). It costs the window, not the table, but still re-validates every row of the window each tick (about 30k rows for 6 h at 5000/h); a per-signature skip for already-dispatched hints would cut that further but weakens the "integrity is mandatory" rule, so it was not done.
- T22F's allow-list was not available (T22 not DONE): producer integrity codes are PERMANENT because they are unlisted. Revisit when T22F lands. Other T01 NORMAL blockers (budget exhaustion, history page limit, retained witness required) stay PERMANENT.
- The dispatcher reports table pressure in its own result only; the health check (not in OWNS) does not read it yet. `desk.watchlist.capacity_pressure()` is the hook.
- The dispatcher-level end-to-end test cannot cross a two-hour gap (the fixture's provider data fails with `HISTORY_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`), so it shrinks the fresh window; the unit and boundary tests cover the real 7200/21600/900 numbers.
- The empty-window scenario is proven with the real T01 result at the classification and reconcile boundaries plus the existing 30-minute end-to-end admission test; a single dispatcher run that produces a real producer-blocked pass is not possible in this harness (its cycle is patched to the fixture's event builder).
