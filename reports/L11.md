## What it does (`lean/held_risk.py`)
Per held mint, in the position loop:
* **(a) LIQUIDITY_DROP**: the quote vault fell by MORE than `rug_liq_drop_frac` (0.7) since entry. Baseline = `quote_gross_raw` of the screen that passed
  (the same units as the vault read), else the first mark.
* **(b) FREEZE**: the mint's freeze authority is set (COption tag, bytes 46..50). Recorded (`freeze_flag`, once) and reported. It forces an exit only when
  `freeze_forces_exit` is true (default false): the task lists only (a), (c), (d) as forced exits. A paper desk holds no token account, so "the account is
  frozen" cannot be observed; a later freeze shows up as (c) when routes stop quoting.
* **(c) NO_ROUTE**: a full-size Jupiter sell quote on the exit lane every `route_probe_s` (60 s). A non-transient failure starts a persisted clock; success clears it;
  after `unsellable_after_s` (120 s) it triggers. A provider OUTAGE (timeout, 5xx, 429) is never "no route". A probe keeps its raw bytes only when it fails.
* **(d) POOL_GONE**: a vault account is missing from the marks read, the pool account is closed, or its owner program differs from the first one read (migrated).
  One batched `getMultipleAccounts([mint, pool, ...])` per ≤50 positions every `freeze_check_every` (6) position passes also serves (b).

On (a), (c), (d): sell the whole position at a fresh quote q1 on the exit lane, exit reason `RUG_EXIT` (trigger recorded in the closed state and the decision), cooldown as
after a stop. If no executable route exists (a non-transient quote failure, or a route whose net value is ≤ 0 after slippage and the fixed fee) the position is
**UNSELLABLE**: valued in the equity at the best executable quote seen (the last good probe), or **0** if there never was one; re-tried every pass (a route that
comes back is used, trigger `ROUTE_RESTORED`); after `unsellable_writeoff_s` (6 h from the moment it became UNSELLABLE) it is closed in the books at that value with
exit reason **`RUG_WRITEOFF`**: a sell fill with fee 0 and slippage 0 (nothing is sent), `realized = value - cost`, labelled EXECUTION_UNVERIFIED, through `store.add_fill`
(so `apply_fill`'s reconciliation and the invariants apply). A transient failure of the exit quote is retried, never turned into UNSELLABLE.

**State and restart.** Generic `events` rows of kind `held_risk` (`baseline`, `pool_owner`, `no_route`, `route_ok`, `trigger`, `freeze_flag`, `unsellable`, `closed`),
keyed by `(mint, open_fill_id)`, replayed at start. No schema change (a lean store is never migrated). UNSELLABLE, the no-route clock and the write-off clock survive a
restart (tested: the write-off lands 6 h after the ORIGINAL `since`, not 6 h after the restart).

**Report** (`lean/report.py`, additive): "Rugs and write-offs": counts and PnL of `RUG_EXIT` and `RUG_WRITEOFF` apart from the ordinary exit reasons, the triggers that fired,
freeze flags, and the positions UNSELLABLE now with their value. `health.json` gets a `held_risk` block.

## Limitations
* **Probe cost**: one Jupiter call per held position per `route_probe_s` plus one batched Helius read every 6 passes. With 4 positions about 4 Jupiter calls a minute on the exit lane.
* **(b) is a flag**, not an exit, by default (see above). "Used" freeze (a holder frozen) is not observable in a paper desk.
* **Baseline** is the reserve at the screen, not at the fill (seconds apart); a rug between them is invisible to (a) but the exit quote then reflects it.
* **Mark accuracy** for boosted pools (virtual reserves) is L09's limitation and affects (a) only through the baseline units, which are the vault's gross amount on both sides.
* **UNSELLABLE counts as an open position** for caps and exposure until it is written off (its cost basis stays in exposure); this is the conservative reading.
* **Not run**: the desk suite (`desk/` and non-lean tests are untouched). Untested against live services (shapes follow L09's fixtures).

## For the coordinator
* Defaults are conservative and documented; to see the feature working without waiting for a rug, lower `route_probe_s`/`unsellable_after_s` in a test config.
* If you want (b) to exit by default, set `freeze_forces_exit: true`.
