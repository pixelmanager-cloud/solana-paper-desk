## What changed (`desk/engine.py`, +87 lines, all behind `paper_portfolio_risk_version: 1`)
- `_portfolio_risk(cfg)`: validates the flag and the four required parameters, fail closed (also on parameters present **without** the flag, so a typo cannot silently disable a gate). Called at the top of `transition`, so even a control event on a bad config raises.
- **Aggregate open risk** `<= X` of equity: `open_risk = sum(cost_left * max(0, 1 - stop_ratio))`; the entry amount is clamped to `(X*equity - open_risk)/stop_fraction - fixed_fee` (9 dp, rounded down). Below `min_order_sol` the entry is refused as `PORTFOLIO_RISK_CAP` (record carries `size_sol`, `open_risk`, `limit`). Positions whose stop moved to breakeven or above (after a take-profit rung) contribute 0.
- **Loss-streak cooldown**: the engine's existing `loss_streak` (full closes only) at `>= N` sets `portfolio_cooldown_until = exit_ts + M*60`, hooked at both streak-update sites (`sell` and `_quote_sell`). Entries refused as `PORTFOLIO_LOSS_COOLDOWN`; exits unaffected.
- **Entry spacing**: at most K entries per rolling 600 s (`portfolio_entries` holds at most K timestamps), refused as `PORTFOLIO_ENTRY_SPACING`; refusals consume nothing.
- `portfolio_blockers(state, cfg, ts)` and `portfolio_open_risk(state)`: pure helpers for entry paths and reporting.
- Daily pause/liquidation, `max_positions`, exposure, per-mint cooldown, throttle, stale-portfolio and the existing `loss_streak` 4/6 rules are untouched and keep label precedence (portfolio reasons are appended last).
- With the flag absent no state key is written and a 16-event scenario (entries, throttle reject, stop and danger exits, controls, clock expiry) produces the **same digest as the engine before this change** (`765bd699...c505f494`, computed on the pre-change engine and pinned in the test).

## Remaining limitations
- The quote-mode exit hook (`_quote_sell`) is verified structurally (AST) and by sharing the helper, not by a full quote-mode lifecycle run; those fixtures are heavy.
- The risk limit uses equity at the entry decision, so the book can exceed `X * equity_after_fill` by roughly the new fill's cost (a few 1e-4 SOL); the next entry then sees no room.
- `portfolio_cooldown_until` is advanced by *any* full losing close at `>= N`, including liquidation closes.
- The pre-check (`portfolio_blockers`) is not wired into the entry paths, so a doomed candidate still costs its investigation requests before the engine refuses it.
