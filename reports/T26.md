## What changed and why
- `tools/research/counterfactual.py` (new CLI `init | ingest | sample | report | set-allowance`): own append-only `counterfactual.sqlite` (trigger-guarded on every table, 0600). Candidates come from `discovery/continuous.sqlite` (`mode=ro`, same `decode` + `_migration_event_hint` selection as the dispatcher; a row whose stored hash does not match its payload is skipped and counted, never learned from). Dispatcher outcome/rejection codes are joined read-only from the dispatch journal and appended to `outcomes` only when they change.
- Sampling at +5m/+15m/+30m/+1h/+2h/+6h: one batched `getMultipleAccounts` per up to 50 candidates (two vault accounts each); the vault addresses are resolved once per pool from the verified PumpSwap pool account (`desk.pools.parse_pool`, base mint and SOL quote checked). Price = constant-product spot `quote_vault/base_vault`; vault owner program, mint and pool authority are verified or the sample is `FAILED` (never priced). Closed/zero vault = `POOL_DEAD`. A slot later than `grace_seconds` is recorded `MISSED`, never sampled late.
- Own allowance (default 300/hour, recorded in append-only `policy`; changed only by appending). Every request is inserted in `requests` BEFORE I/O (so a kill still counts), with its outcome in `request_results`; failures are charged. Real transport (`HeliusTransport`) goes through the shared provider pacing (`acquire/finish/throttle`, investigation priority), refuses without `DESK_PROVIDER_PACING_DB` or a credential, bounds the response, forbids redirects and fails closed on any malformed envelope.
- Metrics: max gain, max drawdown, return and liquidity per horizon, pool death. Report: per rejection code (and `ADMITTED:<status>`, `NOT_DISPATCHED`, `UNKNOWN_OUTCOME`) mean/median/p10/p90 return, share positive, deaths, median max gain.
- `deploy/fresh/desk-counterfactual.service` (inactive template, read-only paths except its own store root), `docs/research/COUNTERFACTUAL.md`.
- No file under `desk/` was edited; the implementation hash is unchanged.

## Remaining limitations
- Baseline for returns is the first priced sample (+5m): the migration-time price is not observed. Reports say so.
- Constant-product spot from vault balances ignores accrued protocol/creator fees and virtual reserves (small for nonboosted pools); it is a research price, not an executable one.
- Decision-store outcomes are not joined (the dispatcher journal already carries the typed blockers); say if the decision store should be a second source.
- The real transport and systemd unit were not exercised against Helius (no provider calls allowed); the pacing integration is by construction from `paper_read_sources`. Coordinator should run `init` + one supervised `sample` after deploy.
- Slots later than the grace window are skipped, so a long outage leaves gaps (recorded as `MISSED`).
- Ingest scans at most 500 discovery rows per run (cursor in `meta`).
