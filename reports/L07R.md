# L07R: a shared low-priority provider lane, and the price-path recorder wired into the integrated runner

Branch `cloud/L07R` is based on `origin/integration/r1` (L09, L09b, L17, entry spacing). `desk/` is untouched. PAPER
ONLY: nothing signs or broadcasts. The recorder only reads pool vault accounts.

## Result
`python -m unittest discover -s tests/lean -t .` in one process: **361 tests, OK, real rc=0** (about 4.4 s).
- That is the 331 tests of `integration/r1` plus 30 new ones:
  - `test_low_lane.py`: 12
  - `test_paths.py`: 15
  - `test_e2e_paths.py`: 3
- `test_e2e_real.py` passes unchanged. It now runs with the recorder enabled, because the example config turns it on.
- No network is used: `socket.connect` is patched to fail and all HTTP goes through fake openers. Fake keys only.

## Part 1: the shared `low` lane (`lean/providers.py`)
- **Budget split.** Every provider's plan rate is split into three buckets: main 0.55, exit 0.25, low 0.20.
  - Helius (10/s): main 5.5/s, exit 2.5/s, low 2/s.
  - Jupiter (4/s): 2.2 / 1 / 0.8.
  - Kraken keeps one bucket and has no low lane.
- **Configurable and validated.**
  - `lean.json` key `lanes` sets the shares plus `low_shed_s`; when it is absent, the defaults above apply.
  - `providers.configure_lanes(shares, low_shed_s=)` is called by `build_runner` before any client is built.
  - The shares must be exactly main, exit and low. Each must be a number in (0, 1], and they must sum to ≤ 1.
  - A refused config changes nothing.
- **Non-blocking (`LowLaneLimiter`).**
  - `acquire` never sleeps. Without a token right now, it raises `ProviderError('LANE_SHED', transient=True)` with
    `meta.why='no_token'`; inside a shed window, `why='backoff'`. Nothing is sent in either case.
  - Low-lane transports never retry (`max_attempts=1`), so nothing on the lane ever waits.
- **429 shedding.** An HTTP 429 seen by ANY lane (main, exit or low) sheds the provider's low lane for
  `max(low_shed_s, Retry-After)`. The hook in `Transport.call` is delimited `# --- L07R hook ---`.
- **Exits never wait behind low traffic.** The lanes are separate buckets. Two tests check this:
  - 1,000 low-lane calls at one instant: 2 go out and 998 are shed, nothing ever sleeps, the exit bucket is untouched,
    and exit calls go out with zero wait.
  - 8 threads flood the low lane on a real clock, and timed exit calls stay immediate.
- **API for other workers.**
  - `providers.low(keys)` is the same as `build_providers(keys, lane='low')`, with `kraken=None`.
  - `providers.is_shed(error)`, `providers.low_lane_stats(provider)` and `providers.shed_low(provider, s)`.
  - These are documented in `docs/lean/README.md` ("Provider lanes"), with a usage snippet.
- **Behaviour change to note.** The main lane drops from 0.75 to 0.55 of the budget (Helius 7.5/s to 5.5/s,
  Jupiter 3/s to 2.2/s). Screening needs 3 Helius calls per candidate, so this is far from binding at the
  observed candidate rate. The exit lane is unchanged at 0.25.

## Part 2: the path recorder (`lean/paths.py`)
- **Which candidates.** Every candidate that passes the HAZARD checks, entered or not:
  - entered candidates;
  - screen rejects ONLY for market cap or liquidity out of band;
  - passed screens that were not entered: the cost cap, portfolio limits (MAX_POSITIONS etc.), the entry throttle, a
    cooldown, a failed quote (`ENTRY_FAILED:<code>`), or entries stopped.
  - Hazard rejects and failed screens are never recorded.
  - A candidate in the retry queue is decided on its final attempt, so the path is started once with the true outcome.
- **Rows** (append-only `observations`):
  - `path_start` holds the screen features, `entered`, `not_entered: {stage, reasons}`, `holders_checked` and the
    `target`. The target is the vault keys, decimals, screen-time pool fees, and the reference quantity and its cost.
  - `path_mark` holds `{mint, ts, slot, base_raw, quote_raw, net_sol, liquidity_sol, status}`.
    - `raw` is the vault amounts and slot as canonical JSON. The meta holds everything.
    - `net_sol` is `adapters.mark(qty, base_raw, quote_raw, pool_fee_bps, pcfg)`, the function the live position loop
      uses. The e2e test checks that it EQUALS the runner's live vault mark at the same instant (116 pairs).
    - The quantity is the filled quantity for an entered candidate, else the tokens `ref_size_sol` (0.2 SOL) buys at
      the path-start reserves (`basis: fill | ref_size`).
    - `liquidity_sol` = 2 × (quote vault − pool fees seen at the screen).
    - `status` is OK, ACCOUNT_MISSING, MALFORMED or EMPTY_POOL.
  - `path_end` is written after `path_hours` (`COMPLETE`), or `EXPIRED_WHILE_DOWN` on resume.
- **Polling.**
  - One `getMultipleAccounts` per ≤ 100 accounts, which is 50 pools, on the LOW lane.
  - It runs every `interval_s` (15 s) in its own daemon thread, started from `Runner.run()` and joined with a 30 s bound.
  - The marks of one poll are written in ONE store transaction (`Store.add_observations`, a new additive method with
    the same row format as `add_observation`).
  - No provider I/O happens under any lock: the recorder's own lock guards only memory, and no runner lock is taken.
- **Pacing.** The low lane's burst is 2. When it has no token, the recorder's own thread sleeps one token interval
  (0.5 s) and continues, within 80% of the interval, so 9 calls spread over about 4 s. A 429 shed is never waited:
  the rest of the poll is counted as gaps.
- **Restart.** The event `paths_active` (`store.record`) holds the live `path_start` ids. It is rewritten on every
  start and end. `build_runner` calls `resume()` before any loop runs, using `store.latest_event` and
  `store.observation`, which are existing store APIs. Ended paths get their `path_end`.
- **Failures.** Store, provider and parse failures are counted in `health.json` → `paths` and never raised:
  `errors_by_code`, `gaps`, `shed`, `paced`, `write_failures`, `hazard_skipped`, `duplicates`, `capacity_dropped`,
  `resumed`, and `low_lane` counters. A recorder failure never reaches entries or exits.
- **Config.** `lean.json` key `paths` = `{enabled, path_hours, interval_s, ref_size_sol, max_paths}`.
  - When the key is absent, the recorder is **disabled**. `config/lean/lean.example.json` sets `enabled: true`.
  - Unknown keys and out-of-range values are refused.
- **Runner hooks** (additive, inside `# --- L07R hook ---` blocks): `self.paths` / `_l07r_screened` in `__init__`; one
  line after the screen decision; one call at the end of `_isolated` plus the `_l07r_after` method; the thread in
  `run()`; and `paths` in `health()`.
  - `build_runner` and `load_config` got delimited L07R blocks.
  - `store.py` got one delimited additive method.

### Deviation from the spec: 250 pools take 5 calls, not 3
`getMultipleAccounts` accepts at most **100 accounts** (`MAX_MULTIPLE_ACCOUNTS`). A pool's reserves are in two vault
accounts (the PumpSwap pool account holds none), so a call can cover 50 pools at most. 250 pools therefore take 5 calls,
each of 100 accounts. That is what `test_250_pools_are_5_calls_of_at_most_100_accounts` asserts. "3 calls" would need
one account per pool, and no such account exists.

### Known limitations
- Band rejects stop the screen before the holder check, so their paths carry `holders_checked: false`. Their hazard
  check is complete except for holder concentration.
- `liquidity_sol` subtracts the pool fees seen at the screen. Fees that accrue later are not re-read (that would cost a
  third account per pool).
- Paths are not deduplicated across restarts for a mint the runner re-screens. The runner itself never re-screens a
  handled mint (`store.candidate_id`).

## Tests and mutations
- **`test_low_lane.py`:** defaults and validation; the low lane fails fast and never sleeps; a shed call sends nothing;
  no retries; the deterministic flood and the threaded flood never delay the exit lane; a 429 on main, exit or low sheds
  the low lane (and Retry-After extends it) without blocking main or exit.
- **`test_paths.py`:**
  - batching: 250 pools → 5 calls of ≤ 100 accounts, all pools marked in one poll and paced;
  - the mark is `adapters.mark` of the reference or fill quantity; closed or empty pools get a status;
  - shedding under 429: one request, then shed with no sends and no sleeps, resuming after the window; a 429 on the main
    lane also sheds the recorder;
  - restart resume, including a path that expired while down, and a bad or missing checkpoint;
  - start and stop timing: 240 marks in 1 h, `path_end` at exactly +1 h, no polls after;
  - rows are append-only: earlier rows are byte-identical, and UPDATE/DELETE are refused;
  - eligibility, entry outcome, failure isolation and config.
- **`test_e2e_paths.py`** (fakeworld with the real modules):
  - The test_e2e_real scenario runs with the recorder off and on. Fills, decisions, exits and cash are **identical**,
    and the exits equal test_e2e_real's.
  - Paths are recorded for the 7 entered candidates and 9 soft rejects: mcap_high, liquidity_low, cost, full_1..5 and
    no_route. None are recorded for the 4 hazards.
  - Path marks equal the live marks. The restart resumes exactly the 9 live paths.
  - The Helius outage is counted (`HTTP_503`) in the recorder's health.
  - A Jupiter outage during a quote is decided once, on the retry.
  - `run()` starts the recorder thread, and an absent `paths` key disables it.

Each mutation was applied in turn, and the full suite was run (rc=1 = killed). **10 of 10 were killed:**

## Cost at the default settings (~70 recorded candidates per hour)
- **Live paths:** 70/h × 6 h = **about 420 at steady state.** The count ramps up linearly over the first 6 h after a
  start.
- **Calls:** ⌈420 / 50⌉ = 9 calls per poll × 240 polls/h = **about 2,160 Helius `getMultipleAccounts` calls per
  hour.**
  - That is about 0.6 req/s on average, 30% of the 2 req/s low lane. Each poll's 9 calls are paced over about 4 s.
  - At 1 credit per standard RPC call (Helius pricing; not verified against this account's plan), that is about 2,160
    credits/h, about 52k/day and about 1.56M per 30 days.
  - If fewer than 70/h pass the hazard checks, the cost scales down linearly. At 35/h it is about 1,200 calls/h.
- **Storage (measured):** about 650 B per `path_mark` row, with a 66-character `code_version`.
  - 420 paths × 240 polls/h ≈ 100,800 rows/h ≈ **65 MB/h, about 1.6 GB/day** of `lean.sqlite` growth.
  - The batched write takes about 4 ms per poll.
  - **This is the main operational cost: check VPS disk headroom before enabling.** Ways to cut it: `interval_s: 30`
    halves it; `path_hours: 3` halves it; a short `LEAN_CODE_VERSION` saves about 60 B per row.
- **Bandwidth (estimated):** about 35 KB per 100-account response (165-byte accounts, base64 in JSON), about 75 MB/h
  downloaded.
