## Items
1. **Pass inventory** (`desk/pass_inventory.py`, new; table `paper_pass_inventory`). One row per completed original pass: `kind`, `scan_id`, `page_intent_hash`
   of its outcome page plus a row digest. Written by the two receipt publishers in the SAME transaction as the outcome
   (`history_preparation_rejection.publish`, `paper_cycle_no_entry.publish`) and, best effort on its own non-waiting connection, by the first
   gate call that meets a completed pass nobody indexed (outcomes written by `paper_cycle`, `paper_pass_closure`, `paper_history_preparation`,
   `paper_observe_cli` or by older code: those files are outside OWNS and unchanged). Append-only triggers, id = count + 1, at most 16384 rows,
   schema compared byte for byte. A gate call now loads pages only for passes NEW since the last call plus `SAMPLE` = 8 pseudo-random rows and the
   newest 2, re-classified from the retained page and compared with the row; everything else is SQL scalars (every row joins to a pass with the same
   intent and outcome hash, contiguity, the digest of each listed receipt-kind row). Both gates use it; `terminal._passes` bound, `bound == retired`
   checks and the two-way inventory are unchanged. Review/pin plans (`review_source` set) and read-only stores never write it.
   Receipts too: of the indexed receipts that are not replayed, `verified_index.QUICK_SAMPLE` = 8 get the page-level binding check per call, the others a
   primary-key presence check of the outcome page (`verified_index.split_quick`, `missing_pages`).
2. **Monitoring chain head** (`paper_monitor_accounting_chain_head`, one row). "Every link up to `seq` was verified"; triggers allow it to move forward only
   and only onto the stored hash of an existing link; written in `_chain_append`'s transaction. `_chain_state` recomputes only the links after the head, a
   sample of `CHAIN_HASH_SAMPLE` = 16 older links from their scalars, and `_to_prove` re-proves from blobs only the reservations the chain does not cover
   plus `CHAIN_SAMPLE` = 2 looked up by link number. A head without its chain, or beyond it, is refused; a chain without a head (T24R stores) is fully verified and
   gains a head at its next completion; `DESK_CHAIN_FULL_VERIFY=1` recomputes everything. Running totals: `total_used`/`high_water` are the stored values of
   `paper_monitoring_budget` (unchanged); the window count stays a SQL scalar count (about 0.1 ms per 1,000 reservations). I did not add per-window total tables:
   they would duplicate those values without being verifiable by SQL.
   **Property test:** `tests/test_monitoring_chain_incremental.py`, 230 seeded random sequences mixing ok / transient / latching reads (chained), abandoned reads
   (resolved without a link) and pending reservations, reopening the database between operations. After EVERY step the incremental result
   (`_chain_state`, `_to_prove`, the stored head) equals a reference written in the test with `hashlib`/`json` only (pinned to the module's golden vector),
   which shares no code with `monitoring_budget`.
3. **Warnings** (`tools/ops/healthcheck.py`, check `capacity`): WARN at `rotation_warn_fraction` (0.8) of the rejection cap (512), the no-entry table (8192),
   closure rows and original passes (10,000, the real ceiling: the task text says 8192 for closure, the code's closure ceiling is 10,000 and 8192 is the
   no-entry table), the inventory (16384), evidence pages (100,000) and evidence bytes (256 MiB, from the file size); for every `publish_refused_v1` row (count, last
   blocker/pass), every `rotation_warning_v1` row, malformed lines and a log at 80 % of its 1 MiB limit. A symlinked log is a failed probe (CRITICAL), as for databases. Read-only.
4. **Benchmark** (`tools/research/bench_history_scale.py`): grows PASSES, RECEIPTS, pages and reads; `--fresh` runs every scale in a new interpreter; `--check` exits 1
   unless loads are flat (exact; +-2 for the "one more pass" call) and seconds bounded. See `docs/PERFORMANCE_BUDGETS.md` for the table. Warm gate: 42 loads and 8 replays at
   17 / 161 / 481 receipts and 218 / 2,162 / 6,482 passes; 0.16-0.20 s. The first call on an existing store classifies every completed pass once (~1 ms each:
   6.6 s at 6,500 passes) and remembers them.
5. **F9 worst case** (`desk/paper_cycle.py`, constants and `worst_case_seconds` only): sized on the 18-read per-scan MAXIMUM (17 + 1 Kraken), with `contenders`
   other units queued on the shared pacer (`CONTENDING_UNITS` = 2) and `GATE_CALLS` x `GATE_SECONDS_BUDGET` for the unit step. Honest consequence: a maximum pass fits the
   10 s default only when responses take <= ~0.2 s uncontended, and the 20 s ceiling when <= ~0.76 s; contended, not even L = 0 fits 10 s. A typical pass reads far less,
   and an overrun is the allow-listed `CYCLE_DEADLINE_UNAVAILABLE`, so these are bounds, not predictions; I did NOT change the deadline range. Units: held 4 x (20 + 1) + 20 = 104 <= 120 s;
   entry 18 + 2 + 20 + 5 + 60 <= 600; the decisions unit (60 s) does no provider I/O (`PrivateNetwork=true`) and its own waits are 5 s (journal init) + 15 s (busy timeout).
6. **Test hygiene**: the unclosed `open()` in `tests/test_cycle_deadline.py` is `Path.read_text()`; every `setUp()` inside a `subTest` in the files I touched now calls
   `doCleanups()` first (`test_monitoring_chain`, `test_t22i`).

## What is no longer re-proved every call (stated plainly)
* A completed pass that was verified once and whose OUTCOME PAGE is later altered or deleted is found when it is sampled (every row over time), by a full replay
  (`DESK_GATE_FULL_REPLAY=1`), or by the coordinator's full verification; its INTENT/OUTCOME hashes in `paper_observation_passes` are still compared with the inventory by SQL on every call.
* A receipt outside the replay and quick samples is not decoded each call: its index digest, pass/scan/intent/outcome binding and the PRESENCE of its outcome page are.
* A monitoring chain link at or before the stored head is not re-hashed each call except the sample; new links always are. A reservation/outcome row edited after it was verified is
  found by the sample or `DESK_CHAIN_FULL_VERIFY=1`. The T24R tests that relied on small stores being fully re-hashed now force the full verification explicitly.
* Everything unindexed, new, or from an older store is still proved in full, as before.

## Limitations
* The original-pass ceiling (10,000, `terminal._passes`) is now the binding limit: 288 passes a day reaches it in ~34 days; the healthcheck warns at 8,000 and the benchmark refuses more than 9,000 passes. Raising it
  is a policy decision outside this task (every closure/no-entry bound is defined relative to it).
* Per-window running totals are not stored (see item 2); the window count is a SQL scalar count.
* Other outcome writers do not record the inventory in their own transaction (outside OWNS); the first gate call after them does (one load per pass).
* Receipt clones in the benchmark have their FULL replay stubbed; their binding checks are real.
* The F9 table uses planning figures (cadences, contenders = 2, 5 gate calls, 1 s gate budget), not measurements of production latency.
* `desk/paper_cycle.py` (constants + `worst_case_seconds`), `desk/paper_pass_closure.py` (`_consistent`) and `desk/verified_index.py` are outside the OWNS list; each edit is small and listed here.
