# L20: sellability evidence for paper SELLs and bot-pump / order-flow features at decision time

Branch `cloud/L20`, base `origin/integration/r1` (`8c64fc8`). PAPER ONLY: read-only RPC on the shared LOW lane, no signing, no sending.
`desk/` untouched. Both features are OFF when their lean.json key is absent; `config/lean/lean.example.json` turns them on.

## 1. Sellability evidence (`lean/sell_evidence.py`, hooks in `lean/execution.py`)

**Question asked before every paper SELL (TP rung or exit):** did at least `min_organic_sells` (2) DISTINCT signers successfully sell
into this pool in the last `sell_window_s` (300 s)? A sell = a transaction in which the pool's token vault rose and its WSOL vault fell,
and the fee payer's token balance went down while its SOL + WSOL went up (the tx fee is added back). Failed transactions, the paper
taker (`PAPER_TAKER`) and the coin creator (`coin_creator` of the PumpSwap pool account the screen already retained,
`screen:accounts_mint_pool`; parsed with `lean.features.dev_wallet`) never count. The swap parser is `lean.flow.parse_trade`, built on
L13's `_token_amounts` / `parse_signatures` (L13's `parse_swap_tx` only sees buyers and no SOL side, so it did not fit as is).

**Probe:** ONE `getSignaturesForAddress(pool, limit 1000)` + at most `max_tx_samples` (8) `getTransaction`, one per equal time stratum
of the window, the row with the smallest sha256(signature) in each stratum (deterministic and aperiodic: an even spread aliases with a
bot that trades buy-sell-sell and can sample only its buys; the e2e caught exactly that). It stops as soon as enough distinct sellers
are found.

**Verdicts and booking (one fill per exit, one accounting path):**

| verdict | when | booked as |
|---|---|---|
| VERIFIED | >= 2 distinct organic sellers seen | the normal paper SELL; payload `sell_evidence={verdict, sellers, sampled, window_txs, ...}` |
| UNVERIFIED | the probe COMPLETED (every planned sample read, nothing shed) and found fewer | `sell_status: SELL_UNVERIFIED`; policy `zero` (default): `paper.write_off(position, ts, qty_raw=the decided qty)`: 0 proceeds, 0 fee, average cost booked as the loss; policy `flag_only`: the normal SELL at q1, flagged |
| UNKNOWN | shed / provider error / sample incomplete / cap / not finished at fill time | the normal SELL; payload `sell_evidence: "UNKNOWN"`, `sell_evidence_why` |

**Design choice (never delay an exit, single accounting path):** the probe runs INSIDE the L10 two-phase window. When an exit defers in
phase 1, `Execution.run_exits` starts the probe on a daemon thread (`SellEvidence.start` returns at once; cache hit / in-flight probe
reused). Before its one shared sleep the pass waits for the probes for AT MOST the remaining delay (real time), then sleeps whatever is
left, so the exit happens at exactly the same instant as without L20. Phase 2 reads the verdict without waiting (`PENDING` -> UNKNOWN);
a late result still fills the cache. The alternative ("book, then a correcting write-off fill") was rejected: a sell already booked
cannot be clawed back through `apply_fill` without a new accounting path. The UNVERIFIED-zero fill is built by the existing write-off
constructor (`paper.write_off`, extended with an optional `qty_raw` for a partial TP rung; omitted = exactly the old D2 behaviour) and
applied by the store's one `apply_fill`; the payload is a `write_off` payload (`write_off_reason: SELL_UNVERIFIED`, the quote's proceeds
kept as `unverified_quote_sol_lamports`). A full unverified close writes the ATA rent off (the account cannot be closed with unsellable
tokens in it), so the outstanding rent still nets to 0; a partial one carries its share of the rent basis, which `execution.economic`
now strips from write-offs too (D2 write-offs have basis 0: unchanged). The serial fallback path (`after_exit_quote` outside
`run_exits`) does the same inside its own delay. `sell_evidence.enabled` without `execution` is refused at load.

**Cache and budget:** conclusive results (VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED) cached per pool for `cache_s` (60 s); UNKNOWN never cached; one probe
in flight per pool. Hard cap `max_calls_per_hour` = 400 (sliding hour, only requests actually sent count; a LANE_SHED is refunded).

**Report / notify:** `lean/report.py` has a "Sell evidence" section (sells per verdict, UNKNOWN reasons, every UNVERIFIED sell with
booked vs quoted proceeds and the total forgone SOL) and tags unverified fills `SELL_UNVERIFIED:<reason>` in the exit-reason table.
The notify SELL message appends `⚠️ 매도 검증 안됨` (plus `(0 SOL로 처리)` under policy zero). Health has `sell_evidence` counters.

## 2. Order-flow / bot-pump features (`lean/flow.py`, `observations(kind='flow')`)

One `flow_v1` row per screen PASS decision (re-screens included), its own daemon thread (`Runner.run`, L20 hook), pending decisions read
from the store (restart-safe by `decision_id`). Window: whole block-time seconds `[ceil(decision_ts - window_s), floor(decision_ts))`,
clipped at graduation (`migrated_at`); a tx is used only when its whole second ended before the decision; the rate's span is the same
interval. Rows carry `decision_ts`, `collected_at`, `late_s`, `window_after_decision_s` (0.0), `flags`, `unavailable` (every null has a
reason), `calls`.

| field | from |
|---|---|
| `tx_rate_per_s` | every successful signature in the window / span (flag `TRUNCATED_SPAN` if the pages did not reach the start) |
| `buy_sell_ratio`, `unique_buyers`, `unique_sellers` | sampled swaps (flag `SAMPLE`; buys / max(1, sells)) |
| `trade_size_cv` | population sd / mean of sampled swap SOL sizes |
| `monotonic_up_frac`, `max_drawdown_pre` | the pool's post-swap spot price (post WSOL / post token vault) over the sample |
| addendum #1 wash trading: `txs_per_unique_wallet`, `roundtrip_wallets`, `roundtrip_share`, `top5_signer_tx_share` | sampled swaps per signer |
| addendum #7 path shape: `return_vol` (sd of per-swap log returns: a sampled pair's log return / the number of pool txs between them), `max_runup_pct`, `longest_up_streak` | the spot sequence |

Sample = `max_txs` (12) swaps, one per stratum (same aperiodic pick as above).

**Learner (`lean/learn.py`):** `FLOW_FEATURES` (= `lean.flow.FEATURES`, 14 fields) added to `FEATURE_NAMES`. Guards: a flow row is used
only if `flow_v1`, its own `decision_ts` <= the decision (`_pick`, the same rule as the other rows), `window_after_decision_s == 0`;
any flag other than `SAMPLE` (e.g. `TRUNCATED_SPAN`) excludes that value; every exclusion is counted. `runner_changes` asks for
`wait_for_flow:<features>` when a proposed rule uses them. Tested with the REAL writer (`FlowCollector.collect` into a real store) and
the REAL L07R2 recorder.

## Call budget (Helius low lane: 10/s x 0.20 = 2/s = 7,200/h, shared with paths / L11 / L12F / L13)

| module | per unit | hard cap | expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| sell_evidence | <= 1 + 8 per probe, 60 s cache per pool | 400 / h | <= 4 positions x ~4 sells each over their life, < 30 probes/h -> < 270 calls/h worst case, usually ~3-5 calls (early stop) |
| flow | <= 3 pages + 12 txs per PASS decision | 900 / h (60 decisions/h) | PASS decisions/h x ~13 |

Together at most 1,300 calls/h (18 % of the low lane, ~1 credit each at the ops estimates -> < 1M credits/month worst case). Both
leave room to the other low-lane users: flow keeps `reserve_tokens` (1) and paces `no_token` sheds on its own clock; the probe paces in
real time on its own thread and never past its deadline.

## Limitations

* **Coverage in production will be partial.** The low lane is 2 tokens/s with a burst of 2 and is shared; a full probe needs up to 9
  calls and only gets the 3 s exit delay. Expect many UNKNOWN (PENDING / LANE_SHED) verdicts, booked at the quote: the report counts
  them so coverage is visible. The 60 s cache helps the next sell of the same pool only. Raising the low share or pre-warming probes
  for positions near a TP would improve coverage (not done: budget decision for CK).
* A quiet pool (no successful sells in 5 minutes) is UNVERIFIED and, under policy `zero`, a legitimate stop on a dead pool is valued
  at 0. That is the literal rule asked for; `flag_only` keeps the quote and only flags it.
* Sampling: at most 8 transactions; a real pool with few sells among many buys can be UNVERIFIED by chance (false positive -> zeroed).
  Distinct signers are not distinct owners: a bot rotating wallets can fake "organic" sellers; a seller that tips heavily in SOL can net
  negative and not count.
* The creator is only known when the screen retained the pool account; otherwise only the taker is excluded.
* Flow: sample figures are from 12 swaps (flag `SAMPLE`; unique counts are lower bounds); the bonding-curve phase before graduation is
  not seen (the pool's history only); a very busy pool with a late collector can be `TRUNCATED_SPAN` or `DECISION_NOT_REACHED`; only
  screen PASS decisions are collected (the learner's candidates). Features only: nothing here changes an entry.
